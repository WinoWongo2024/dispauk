Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

1. Who we are

DispaUK (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the website dispauk.co.uk. We are an independent project and are not affiliated with SHPlay GmbH or MissionChief.

Contact for privacy matters: administrator@dispauk.co.uk

2. What data we collect

We aim to collect the minimum data necessary:

  • Contact information – if you email us (email address and any details you choose to provide).
  • Technical data – basic server logs that may include IP address, browser type, and pages visited (standard web-server logging).
  • Cookie / local storage data – only with your consent where required (see our Cookie Policy).

We do not currently operate user accounts, payment processing, or marketing mailing lists.

3. How we use your data

  • To respond to enquiries you send us.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal bases (UK GDPR)

Where the UK GDPR applies, we process personal data on the following bases:

  • Consent – for non-essential cookies.
  • Legitimate interests – operating and securing the website, and responding to messages you initiate.
  • Legal obligation – where we are required to retain or disclose information by law.

5. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with:

  • Service providers who help us run the site (e.g. web hosting – currently GitHub Pages).
  • Authorities if required by law.

6. International transfers

Our website is hosted via GitHub Pages. Data may be processed in the United States or other locations. Where applicable we rely on appropriate safeguards.

7. Retention

Contact emails are retained only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards. Server logs are retained for a limited period for security and operational purposes.

8. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase data in certain circumstances
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at administrator@dispauk.co.uk.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when changes are made.
