Live mission feed

MissionChief does not publish a stable public API for recent completed missions (units on scene, payout, mission title). Documented APIs cover buildings, vehicles, credits, alliance info, and the mission-type catalogue (einsaetze.json), not a history of individual calls.

When or if a reliable missions history endpoint becomes available, this page will list the last five missions with responding units and credits earned. Until then, credit movement is tracked on the dashboard via hourly snapshots.
