Accessibility statement

Last updated: 29 August 2026

We aim to make DispaUK usable by as many people as possible. The site uses semantic HTML, visible focus styles, keyboard-friendly controls, and respects reduced-motion preferences where practical.

Some areas (especially interactive maps and content loaded from JSON) may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Live regions are used on key status and result areas to announce updates.

If you encounter a barrier, email administrator@dispauk.co.uk with the page URL and what went wrong.
