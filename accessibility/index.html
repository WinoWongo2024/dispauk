Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Our commitment

DispaUK aims to make this website accessible to as many people as possible, including those with disabilities.

Measures we take

  • Semantic HTML structure
  • Sufficient colour contrast where possible
  • Keyboard-navigable interactive elements
  • Visible focus indicators
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences
  • Clear page titles and headings
  • Alternative text and labels where relevant

Known limitations

As a small independent project the site may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA in every area. We continue to improve accessibility over time.

Feedback

If you experience any accessibility barriers on this website, please contact us:

Email: administrator@dispauk.co.uk

Please include the page URL and a description of the problem. We will try to respond and address issues where reasonably possible.

Enforcement

If you are not satisfied with our response you may contact the Equality and Human Rights Commission or seek advice from the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS).
