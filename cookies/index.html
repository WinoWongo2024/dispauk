Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels used by advertising partners.

2. How DispaUK uses cookies

Strictly necessary

Required for the site to function, including remembering your cookie consent choice. These do not require consent under UK PECR.

Preferences / functional

We may store a simple preference (such as your cookie consent decision) in local storage so that we do not show the banner on every visit.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

With your consent, we use Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-1802148026096192) to show advertisements. Google and its partners may set cookies and collect data such as:

  • Pages visited on this site and interaction with ads
  • IP address, device and browser type
  • Identifiers used for ad measurement and (where allowed) personalisation

Google may use this information to show ads on this site and across the web. See Google’s policies: How Google uses data from sites that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can manage advertising via the cookie banner, browser settings, and Google Ad Settings.

3. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through:

  • The cookie banner on this website (Accept / Reject non-essential)
  • Your browser settings (block or delete cookies)
  • Google’s ad settings: adssettings.google.com
  • Industry opt-outs where available (e.g. Your Online Choices)

Blocking all cookies may affect some site features. Blocking advertising cookies will limit or stop personalised ads but the rest of the site should still work.
