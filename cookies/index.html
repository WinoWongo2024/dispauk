Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage and session storage.

2. How DispaUK uses cookies

We use a minimal approach:

Strictly necessary

These are required for the site to function (for example, remembering your cookie consent choice). They do not require consent under UK rules.

Preferences / functional

We may store a simple preference (such as your cookie consent decision) in local storage or a cookie so that we do not show the banner on every visit.

Analytics / non-essential

We do not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. If this changes in the future we will update this policy and request consent before setting non-essential cookies.

3. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through:

  • The cookie banner on this website (accept or reject non-essential cookies).
  • Your browser settings (you can block or delete cookies at any time).

Blocking all cookies may affect some functionality of the site.

4. More information

For details about how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
